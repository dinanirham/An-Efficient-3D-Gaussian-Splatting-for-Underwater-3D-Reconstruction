Research methodology · schematic · as run, campaign 2026-09

Three mechanisms, three insertion points,
one shared variable

The efficiency mechanisms act at disjoint stages and share no code. They meet anyway — at the physical water model, which is re-fitted against whatever geometry the mechanisms leave it. These figures exist to make that coupling visible, because it is what the study measures; the schedule figure exists because the campaign showed when the coupling acts matters more than the pipeline figures alone can show.

The whole method in one cycle. Unmodified Gaussian splatting compares the render directly against the capture. The underwater baseline does not: it reinterprets the render as medium-free radiance and composes it through a physical model before any comparison is made. M1, M2 and M3 attach at two points of that cycle and never touch the medium model — which is exactly why their effect on it was unexpected. D is drawn with a dashed outline because it is a supplementary contrast, not a fourth factor: it modifies which gradients reach density control, and M1 disables density control entirely, so D is provably inert in every M1 cell. A 2⁴ design would be half duplicates. D ran as cell A0D, twelve runs, against A0 alone.

The decision at i > 10 000 is the figure's subject. Below it there is no medium model: the loss compares the raw render to the capture, so veiling haze must be paid for by geometry — which is what recruits the floaters the diagnostics later find. Above it the render is reinterpreted as medium-free radiance and composed through the physical model before any comparison. M1 acts entirely below that line; M2 straddles it; M3 lands above it and touches nothing the medium model reads. The ordering says which mechanism can break the physics — only M2 fires after the medium has converged — and the campaign showed it is not sufficient: M2 with M1 ahead of it has the same ordering and never breaks a channel in 24 runs. What differs is the size of the cut the events make, which is the subject of the next figure.

The two circles marked A are the same point in the flow. A backward edge from the end of the loop to its start would have to travel the length of the training block and cross the phase boundaries, which is precisely the case the on-page connector symbol exists for: the reader matches the two circles by label instead of following a line. The loop is no less present for not being drawn.

Why the two regimes are a branch and not nested blocks. The gates overlap: density control runs until iteration 15 000, which is 5 000 iterations into the physics-aware regime. Drawing the regimes as nested containers would put density control wholly inside the first and misstate the schedule. D inserts on the densification-signal composition, which is why it appears on that branch rather than beside M1, M2 or M3.

Figure 2 — the training schedule, as run, and where the medium model falls

The medium converges from 10 000; the first cut lands 5 000 iterations later. The cut moves no medium parameter — on 48 of 48 M2 runs at both events the nine scalars are identical before and after it. They move during the two hundred medium-only steps drawn in accent immediately after, with geometry frozen: this study's own remedy for the coupling it found, reusing the baseline's block-coordinate discipline. In the campaign that burst did not restore the medium; it was the interval in which the medium fell. Every cut that removed 86–95% of the population lost 40–100% of its attenuation inside it, and nine of twenty-four crossed zero on the blue channel, six of them by the burst's last step. No cut that removed 29–36% — the M1 cells, whose clouds enter the event at ~224 k — lost more than 11%.

The sweep circles are the instrument that tested the registered explanation, and refuted it. Before the campaign the coupling was attributed to the change in cross-frame dispersion of the per-frame depth range across the cut — the medium's global β being a compromise over that distribution. The sweep measures exactly that ratio at every event. It does not separate the runs that collapsed from the runs that did not (U = 79 of 135, p = 0.26), and four M1 runs broadened by 3.5–7.3× without losing a channel. The account is withdrawn; the schedule above is what remains established, and why a large cut moves β is the open question the thesis ends on.

The reference control ran first and was compared against A0 on a margin fixed before it existed (±1 dB, ±0.02 LPIPS, ±30% count): within margin on every scene. Reading the figures together

Figure 0 says the mechanisms are structurally independent of the medium model. Figure 1 says they are not temporally independent of it. Both are true, and the gap between them is what the factorial design measures.

  • M1 lands entirely in the geometry-only regime. Its decay window was originally scheduled there too — a cull deciding what the loss “does not defend” while half the objective did not yet exist. Moving it past the boundary is one of the integration decisions marked CD.
  • M2 straddles the boundary. Its two events fire 5 000 and 10 000 iterations after the medium parameters begin converging — which is why it is the only mechanism that breaks them: 5 of 12 runs alone, 4 of 12 with M3, and 0 of 24 when M1 has already reduced the cloud before the first event.
  • M3 lands last and touches nothing the medium model reads. Position and opacity are never quantized, so the depth map is unaffected — and none of its twelve runs loses a channel. Its efficiency result (2.72× storage, no frame-rate change) was predicted from this structure before it ran and held on every scene. What the structure did not predict: quantising three attributes moves the restored image Ĝ by 17–27 dB while moving the composed image by ~1.4 dB, because the attenuation map scales Ĝ down before any metric sees it.
  • D is not on the factorial at all. It changes which gradients reach density control, and M1 replaces density control with pruning — so D cannot act in any M1 cell. It is measured as a supplementary contrast against the bare baseline (cell A0D, twelve runs): 3–5× fewer primitives, PSNR unresolved, LPIPS resolved worse, no channel lost — a continuous reduction of the population leaves the medium intact where a two-step cut does not.

The diagnostics in stage ④ exist because of stage ③. Every failure the ordering produces is invisible to PSNR, SSIM and LPIPS, which all score the composed image — and a model whose attenuation has collapsed still fits that image. Across the campaign's twelve collapsed-versus-intact comparisons within a cell and scene, the median fidelity difference is 0.8 of the baseline's own noise and the sign is inconsistent; a blue attenuation of −0.05 renders held-out views as well as one of +1.2. The reference control, the depth-range sweep and the Ĝ self-consistency check are in stage ④ for the same reason: each measures something the fidelity metrics cannot.
