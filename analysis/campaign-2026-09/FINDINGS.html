Three efficiency mechanisms,
one physical model, 120 runs

The baseline is vanilla SeaSplat to within a pre-registered margin on every scene. Each mechanism moves the cost it targets beyond seed noise on every scene. The medium model falls during the two hundred steps after every large cut and never after a small one — and the explanation registered for that in advance failed its own test.

Per scene, never pooled; resolved means two standard errors

Scene difficulty spans 7.2 dB and the baseline's own dispersion varies four-fold across scenes, so an effect that resolves on one scene may be invisible on another for reasons of measurement. Every effect below is per scene, in units of that scene's baseline noise. UNRES means below 2 SE — a statement about three seeds, not about the mechanism.

The design's own arithmetic settles one question before any data: the smallest PSNR interaction three seeds can resolve is 0.5–1.6 dB, and the largest PSNR main effect is 0.8 dB. PSNR interactions are undetermined by construction. Interactions are adjudicated on LPIPS and primitive count.

The registered mechanism — and its test

Written before the data: collapse at a simplification event is governed by the change in cross-frame dispersion of the depth range across that event — not by primitive count, not by count ratio. The test: within the M2 cells without M1, does the dispersion ratio at the first event separate the nine runs that collapsed from the fifteen that did not?

The identifiability account is withdrawn, not qualified §5b failed — the plan's own falsification clause, written in full before the campaign, and the one item whose failure changes the thesis's central claim. What replaces it is description, labelled post-hoc, and not promoted to a claim.

§11

Against the archived campaign

A0–A3, four scenes, three seeds in both campaigns. LPIPS replicates on all sixteen comparisons. Count replicates for A1 to three figures — dense initialisation is deterministic — and for A2 within 3%. Twelve of sixteen PSNR comparisons replicate.

On IUI3-RedSea, A0 rose by 0.76 dB and A3 fell by 0.95 dB, non-overlapping ranges both times. The new A0 agrees with vanilla on that scene, so the new figure is the anchored one; but M3's PSNR effect there was +0.35 in the old campaign and is −1.36 in this one, and is downgraded to UNRESOLVED across campaigns. The archived A3 manifest reads -dirty: at least one old A3 run was trained from an uncommitted tree, which is the likeliest home of the discrepancy and cannot be recovered now.

RQ

Research questions

RQ1
Supported. Each mechanism moves its own target cost beyond seed dispersion on every scene. Initialisation carries the only resolved fidelity gains and an intact medium in every run it touches; simplification buys the most reduction and breaks the physics on 9 of 24 runs; quantisation delivers 2.72× storage and nothing else, as forecast.
RQ2
Answered on LPIPS and count; undetermined on PSNR by construction. The mechanisms do not compose additively. Initialisation with simplification is sub-additive on count because the budget binds, and costs less perceptually than the sum of its parts.
RQ3
The coupling is established; the mechanism is open. The registered account — dispersion, not count — failed its pre-written test and is withdrawn. What the data show is that the medium falls during the re-fit after every 86–95% cut and never after a 29–36% one, and that nothing measured before the cut predicts which seed crosses zero.
RQ4
Unchanged from the main-effects campaign: five integration properties, each found by running the pipeline rather than analysing it.
§14

What cannot be concluded

  • Any PSNR interaction. Undetermined by construction at three seeds; several clear 2 SE and flip sign scene to scene, which is what noise at the limit looks like.
  • Why the medium falls. The one registered account is withdrawn. Removal fraction, entering β and entering count are set together by M1 and cannot be separated by this design.
  • Which seed collapses. Reproducible as a rate, unpredictable as an event.
  • Restoration accuracy. The Ĝ instrument shows the two states differ by 17–27 dB and cannot say which is nearer the truth; the far-field location of the difference is inferred from the formation model, not mapped.
  • Anything about operating points. One budget, one codebook size; no ranking across budgets.
  • IUI3-RedSea. The scene on which every mechanism costs perceptual quality, no M2 run collapses, and PSNR does not replicate across campaigns. Every per-scene claim is checked against it before it is generalised.
  • Geometry and Ĝ are one seed per cell.
LEDGER

What changed, ranked as the plan ranked it

1 · failed

The dispersion account is withdrawn

The thesis reverts to the approved framing: a controlled factorial with a measured, reproducible, boundary-aligned coupling and an unexplained failed remedy. The why is open, and the chapter says so in those words.

2 · held

D resolves on count from a sound baseline

3–5× at a resolved but small perceptual cost and an unresolved PSNR cost. The thesis has a positive engineering result.

3 · rate held, reason failed

M1 cells do not collapse

0 of 24 against 9 of 24. The stated reason was the withdrawn account. What can be said: M1's cloud enters the first event at ~224 k, so the budget removes a third, not nine tenths.

4 · held on three scenes

Simplification and quantisation are sub-additive on quality

A smaller population is more sensitive to lossy attribute compression. The compression literature's premise transfers.

5 · held

A0 is vanilla SeaSplat to within the margin

The one that mattered most. Every downstream contrast is anchored to a baseline that matches the published method on the metrics the contrasts use.

retired

Two figures, one instrument reading

The dispersion account; the “−0.107 dB” for D; and this author's reading of the Ĝ gap as latent drift, which the continuous-state check refuted on 14 of 16 runs.
