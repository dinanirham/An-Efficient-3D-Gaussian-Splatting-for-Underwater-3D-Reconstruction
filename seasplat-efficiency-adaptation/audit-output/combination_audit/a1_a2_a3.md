## 🔴 C7-1 — One unrecorded number determines what A7 *is*

Everything about this branch pivots on whether A2 fires, and — as established in
`a1_a2.md` C4-1 — with densification disabled the population can never grow, so A2 is
binding at iteration 15 500 or binding never:

| Condition | A7 collapses to |
|---|---|
| `N_init > 800 000` | **A7 as designed.** A2 prunes the lowest `opacity × scale` Gaussians, partially restoring the prune A1 removed, and hands A3 a cleaner opacity distribution. Coherent. |
| `N_init ≤ 800 000` | **A7 ≡ A5 exactly** (`a1_a3.md`), inheriting C5-1 in full: unpruned α ≈ 0 Gaussians meet opacity quantization with nothing between them. |

`N_init` is set by `roma_init.py`'s `voxel = 0.001` / `matches = 5000` / `nns = 3` /
`num_refs = -1`, and is recorded **nowhere** — `docs/` is empty, the dense `.ply` is
unversioned and unhashed (`a1.md` A1-6), and A2's log line only prints when pruning actually
occurs (`a2.md` A2-4).

**Two of the eight factorial cells (A4, A7) cannot be interpreted until this number is
known**, and a third (A6) depends on the same question for the ordinary densified
population. Diagnosing it costs one unconditional log line and zero retraining
(`refit_recommendations.md` R-1).

## 🟢 C7-2 — In the binding case, A7 is the best-composed branch

If A2 fires, the three mechanisms line up in a defensible order:

1. **A1** supplies dense geometry up front, removing the 15 000-iteration gradient-mediated
   search that densification would otherwise spend `[EDGS: 05-constraints.md D-1]`.
2. **A2** removes the residual — including, by the `L_op` alignment (`a2.md` A2-3), exactly
   the backscatter-only Gaussians SeaSplat's opacity prior suppressed but A1 left in place.
3. **A3** compresses what survives, on a population that has already been count-reduced —
   which is the sequencing compact3d argues for (`a2_a3.md` C6-3): quantize *after* the
   count lever has been pulled, because `xyz` dominates the residual otherwise.

This ordering is correct, and it is worth stating explicitly in the thesis. **It appears to
be accidental** — nothing in the code or the empty `docs/` indicates it was reasoned about —
but it is right.

## 🔴 C7-3 — The compounding conflicts that survive regardless

Independent of C7-1, three defects are present in A7:

| # | Conflict | Inherited from |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Iterations 0 → 15 500 have no population management at all** — no densification, no α-prune, no opacity reset, no reorg. `L_op` accumulates suppressed Gaussians across more than half the run with no executor. A2's budget prune at 15 500 is a *cap*, not a *threshold*, so any α ≈ 0 Gaussians under budget survive to the `.ply` even in the binding case. | A1-1 × A2's budget semantics |
| 2 | **Opacity is quantized post-hoc with no recovery step**, in a model whose opacity semantics carry SeaSplat's medium reasoning. A2 reduces but does not eliminate the near-threshold mass. | A3-3 × A1-1 |
| 3 | **`f_dc` quantized post-hoc**, with `L_gw` and `L_sat` unable to rebalance the aggregate colour statistics they spent the run tuning. Affects restoration quality — SeaSplat's actual contribution — more than it affects the reported PSNR. | A3-2 |

## 🟡 C7-4 — "Full integration" overstates what was integrated

The README describes A7 as the "full integrated framework." The verification above shows it
is a **file-level union with no integration code**: no shared budget between `max_gaussians`
and `k`, no rate–distortion objective, no coordination between A2's prune cadence and A3's
codebook, and no mechanism that makes any one of the three aware of the others.

That is a reasonable ablation construction — and it is what makes the 2³ factorial
interpretable — but "integrated framework" implies a designed composition that does not
exist in the code. Prefer "combined" or "stacked" in the write-up.
